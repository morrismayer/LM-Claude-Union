# Gemini Omni Prompts (5 multimodal generation prompts)

Each prompt is written to produce a short multimodal explainer (image/video +
on-screen text) suitable for repurposing into Reels, Stories, or a LinkedIn
video. Style direction is consistent across all five: warm, neutral daylight,
real trade-workroom aesthetic — no stock-photo gloss, no fabricated logos or
people who don't exist as real staff.

---

**Prompt 1 — Pattern repeat**
"Generate a short multimodal explainer, photoreal style, warm neutral
daylight. Open on a macro shot of a large-pattern upholstery fabric (a bold
floral or wide stripe) laid flat on a wooden cutting table. Slowly reveal a
second panel of the same fabric placed beside the first, with an animated
overlay drawing a horizontal line across both panels to show the pattern
either matching or offset. On-screen text: 'Pattern repeat: matched vs.
mismatched.' No people, no logos, no text beyond the specified caption."

**Prompt 2 — Railroaded goods**
"Generate a short multimodal explainer, photoreal style. Show a fabric bolt
unrolling so the printed pattern runs sideways (horizontally) along the
length of the roll rather than vertically. Cut to a wide drapery panel made
from that same fabric, pattern still running horizontally, hanging seamlessly
across a window without a vertical seam. On-screen text: 'Railroaded fabric:
fewer seams on wide panels.' Neutral daylight, real-workroom aesthetic."

**Prompt 3 — Trim application methods**
"Generate a short side-by-side multimodal comparison. Left half: a strip of
cord trim being glued along a fabric edge with a thin bead of adhesive
visible. Right half: the same trim being sewn into a seam with a sewing
machine, thread visible. On-screen text: 'Glued vs. sewn — choose before
fabrication, not after.' Photoreal, close-up macro framing, neutral lighting."

**Prompt 4 — Fullness ratio**
"Generate a short multimodal explainer showing two identical windows side by
side with drapery hung at different fullness ratios — one flat and sparse,
one full and waved. Animate a simple ratio label (e.g., '2:1' vs. '2.5:1')
appearing above each. On-screen text: 'Fullness ratio: the number that makes
or breaks the look.' Photoreal, soft daylight through the window."

**Prompt 5 — 8-way hand-tied construction**
"Generate a short multimodal explainer of an upholstery frame mid-
construction: coil springs visible, with cord being tied from each spring in
eight directions to the frame, shown as a simple animated diagram overlaid on
the photoreal base shot. On-screen text: '8-way hand-tied: built to perform
the same in year five as day one.' Warm workroom lighting, no people in
frame, focus entirely on the construction detail."
