# Grok Image Prompts (5 still-image generation prompts)

Consistent style note: photoreal, warm neutral daylight, real trade-workroom
aesthetic (not a styled showroom), no logos, no identifiable real people.

---

**Prompt 1 — Fabric swatch stack**
"Photoreal still image: a stacked fan of fabric swatches in a coordinated
palette (mix of solid linen-weight textures, one subtle stripe, one small
jewel-tone pattern) laid on a worn wooden cutting table, raking natural
window light from the left, shallow depth of field, slight grain for
realism. No logos, no text."

**Prompt 2 — Trim detail macro**
"Photoreal macro still image: close-up of tassel fringe trim along the
finished edge of a drapery panel, individual threads visible, soft natural
light, neutral warm-toned fabric background, extremely shallow depth of
field isolating the trim detail."

**Prompt 3 — Workroom wide shot**
"Photoreal wide-angle still image: interior of a roughly 6,000-square-foot
custom drapery and upholstery workroom, fabric bolts on shelving along one
wall, long cutting tables down the center, natural daylight from high
windows, lived-in working space rather than a styled showroom, no people in
frame."

**Prompt 4 — Upholstery frame construction**
"Photoreal still image: an upholstery armchair frame mid-construction,
coil springs visible and individually tied in an 8-way hand-tied pattern,
cord and webbing visible against raw wood frame, warm directional workshop
light, sharp focus on the spring-and-cord detail."

**Prompt 5 — Finished ripplefold drapery vignette**
"Photoreal still image: a single drapery panel on a ripplefold track, hung
in soft full fullness against a sunlit window, fabric forming an even wave
from top to bottom, neutral interior wall behind, natural daylight, no
people, no text overlay."
