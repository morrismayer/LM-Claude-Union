# Grok Imagine Video Prompts (6 x 10-second prompts)

Consistent style note for all six: real custom-drapery-and-upholstery
workroom, natural daylight, no overlaid text or logos, no people unless
noted as hands-only (avoid generating identifiable faces of real staff).

---

**Prompt 1 — Fabric shelving pan**
"10-second shot. Slow lateral pan across floor-to-ceiling fabric bolts on
warehouse shelving in a sunlit workroom. Midway through, a hand reaches in
and unrolls one bolt just enough to show a bold pattern running sideways
along the roll (railroaded fabric). Natural daylight, warm tones, shallow
depth of field, no text overlay."

**Prompt 2 — Trim being sewn**
"10-second macro shot. A sewing machine needle stitches cord trim into the
seam of a cushion cover, thread and trim clearly visible, fabric moving
steadily under the needle. End on a pulled-back shot of the finished trimmed
seam. Close, tactile camera work, soft workroom lighting, hands only, no
visible face."

**Prompt 3 — Ripplefold track gliding open**
"10-second shot, eye-level. A drapery panel on a ripplefold track glides
smoothly open across a sunlit window, carriers visible moving along the
track, fabric forming an even continuous wave as it travels. End on the fully
open position with soft daylight streaming through. No people in frame."

**Prompt 4 — 8-way hand-tied knot**
"10-second macro shot. Hands tie off a spring knot on an upholstery frame as
part of an 8-way hand-tied pattern, cord pulled taut in one direction, camera
holds steady on the tying motion. End with a slow pull-back revealing the
full grid of springs and ties across the frame. Warm directional light,
hands only."

**Prompt 5 — Measuring and cutting**
"10-second shot. A tape measure is laid diagonally across a foam cushion on a
cutting table, a hand marks the measurement in chalk, then a fabric cutting
tool follows the chalked line in one clean pass. Top-down camera angle,
practical workroom lighting, no faces."

**Prompt 6 — Workroom-to-finished-room**
"10-second shot. Start wide on the workroom floor — fabric bolts, cutting
tables, an upholstery frame in progress — then a smooth match-cut transition
to a finished ripplefold drapery panel hanging in a softly lit room, fabric
fully open and catching daylight. No people, no text overlay, warm consistent
color grade across both halves of the shot."
