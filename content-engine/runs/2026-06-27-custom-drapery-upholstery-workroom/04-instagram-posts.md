## IG Post 2

[Visual: drapery panel on ripplefold track, partially open, showing the
even wave]

Ripplefold track or traditional pinch pleat — both can be right. What's
never optional is fullness ratio. Too little fabric relative to the track
length and even beautiful fabric looks flat. Get the ratio right and a
modest fabric looks intentional. We calculate it. We don't eyeball it.
