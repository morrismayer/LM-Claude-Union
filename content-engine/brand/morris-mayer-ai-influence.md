## Voice — AI Consulting Brand

This voice is **different from the Plaza Park voice.** Where Plaza Park is
calm, expert, and trade-insider, the AI brand voice is:

- **Direct and energetic.** Morris's own words from his context brief: "Direct,
  energetic, faith-forward, and practical. No fluff. No corporate language."
- **Faith-forward.** Morris speaks to "real people about real problems and real
  solutions" with an underlying faith-informed frame — not preachy, but present.
- **Teacher, not consultant.** The content positions Morris as a peer who
  figured something out and is sharing it, not an expert selling distance.
- **ENFP-A energy.** Enthusiastic, idea-oriented, connection-driven. Talks
  in examples. Moves fast. Makes abstract tools feel approachable and concrete.
- **Beginner-friendly, not dumbed down.** The target audience is often
  non-technical; Morris's job is to make AI tools feel accessible in the
  first 5 minutes.

### What this voice sounds like

- Opens with a problem the audience is already feeling ("You've got 47 unread
  emails and three proposals sitting in draft")
- Names tools by name and shows what they actually do (not "leverage AI
  solutions")
- Uses his own businesses as worked examples — the cleaning company, the
  workroom, the QuickBooks-to-Airtable automation
- Closes with a specific next step, not a vague invitation

### What it does NOT sound like

- Corporate AI hype ("transform your business," "unlock the power of AI")
- Tech-bro minimalism
- Sales-heavy webinar language
- Generic motivational content with no tactical substance

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Small business owners — especially faith-based entrepreneurs,
blue-collar operators, service businesses (cleaning, trades, hospitality,
real estate). People who are running a business with a small team and too much
on their plate, who suspect AI could help but don't know where to start.

**Secondary:** Other consultants, coaches, and content creators in the faith/
business space who want to learn from a peer who has already built the system.

**Not the audience (for this brand):** Interior designers, hospitality
purchasers, the design trade. That's the Plaza Park audience. Don't cross them.

### Named audience archetypes (from AI Hacks After Dark sessions)

- The overwhelmed owner with 47 unread emails and no time to learn new tools
- The faith-based entrepreneur who wants to do more with what they have
- The blue-collar operator who uses paper processes and is open to change
- The fellow practitioner who wants to teach this to their own clients

## Content Themes for AI Brand

These are the recurring content angles Morris returns to across sessions and
social content:

1. **The daily briefing** — How to use AI to replace the morning inbox-checking
   ritual with a focused ops brief (Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini agents that pull
   schedule, overdue invoices, tasks, priorities)
2. **Small-business automation wins** — Specific workflows: QuickBooks to
   Airtable via Zapier, estimate-to-work-order, fabric receiving log, email
   triage to delegate-ready summaries
3. **Tool comparisons** — Practical, opinionated takes on Claude vs ChatGPT vs
   Gemini vs Perplexity for specific tasks. Not academic; which one to actually
   open.
4. **Faith and AI** — How faith-informed business owners can use AI as a tool
   without losing the human element — delegation, integrity, not replacing
   relationships
5. **Content creation systems** — The same content engine approach Morris uses
   for Plaza Park (blog → derivatives → scheduling → posting cadence)

## Differentiators (for content positioning)

- **Practitioner, not theorist.** Morris runs four real businesses. When he
  says "this automation saves 20 office hours a week," it's from his own
  operations, not a case study.
- **Faith-informed.** Not a posture — it's built into who he is and how he
  talks about business decisions.
- **Show-don't-tell demo format.** Every session is live on screen, not a
  slide deck.
- **Multi-tool fluency.** Not locked into one ecosystem. Morris uses Claude,
  ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Airtable in the same workflow and teaches
  the same.
- **Community over funnel.** AI Hacks After Dark is a Facebook group and a
  Zoom room, not a sales funnel.
